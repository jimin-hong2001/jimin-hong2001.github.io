Applied Energy2025First author

Advanced parking assistance system for solar electric vehicles using 360° VR imaging and real-time solar radiation forecasting

Jimin Hong, Yosoon Choi

A roof-mounted 360° VR camera captures the sky above a parking space, and a smartphone app turns that image into solar access. Combined with live solar radiation forecasts, it predicts how much the car will charge before the driver walks away. Validated against a Solmetric SunEye 210 and on-vehicle panel measurements.

doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2025.126365

Comparative techno-economic assessment of energy storage technologies in an off-grid renewable microgrid for AI data centers

Jimin Hong, Yosoon Choi

$0.1427

A HOMER Pro study of a wind- and PV-led microgrid with hydrogen backup, comparing Li-ion, vanadium redox flow and flywheel storage. Flow batteries win at 0.5–1 MW loads; Li-ion takes over at 1.5 MW and above.
